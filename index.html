CHAPTER 01

An Unnecessarily Complicated Introduction

Apparently, making websites is easier than having conversations nowadays.

This is actually my second attempt. (Never found the courage to send the first one. If you're reading this, then I might have finally found the courage I was looking for. Said courage too many times, huh?)

I was thinking of writing a long introduction, but if you've made it this far, you probably already know who's behind this little project. So, with that out of the way — Math 7 has already been postponed. Let's not postpone this one too.

CHAPTER 02

A Sunday, Somewhere in October

I have no idea where to start, but... How about a Sunday in October last year?

First day of the fifth semester. OOAD class. Arrived early, sat around waiting, and eventually, someone walked into the classroom. Caught my eye, if only for a moment. Didn't think much of it at the time, though. Then came the waiting. And more waiting. Our eventual favourite faculty member never showed up. (Not actually a favourite, obviously. Just an inside joke.) A few weeks went by, and still no faculty.

> Eventually, some brave souls decided enough was enough and went to the department head to find out what was going on. I somehow ended up being the one who went upstairs and came back with the update. Basically, the faculty hadn't joined yet, so we'd probably have to wait a little longer for classes and quizzes. And that, as far as I remember, was our first actual interaction.

Nothing particularly remarkable, just a quick update to a room full of equally frustrated students. I won't pretend I remember every little detail, but it stuck with me. At least,

it did for me.

CHAPTER 03

Who Knew Good Things Could Come From an End?

Who knew good things could come from an ending? Well, this particular ending was the end of a semester.

Last day of the previous semester. AI exam. We were in neighbouring rooms, and I remember noticing you before the exam started. Didn't want to interrupt your preparation, so I just kept to myself, secretly hoping someone would notice me.

Well, someone did.

After the exam, once all the dust had settled, came the usual post-semester tradition: reflecting on how every semester somehow manages to be worse than the last. Then came an upcoming event, and perhaps an opportunity to get a little more acquainted. One thing led to another, a few clicks along the way, and somehow I found myself a little more involved in your world.

And just like that, things started changing.

CHAPTER 04

Little Things, Quiet Nights

Then came the semester break.

What I didn't expect were the late-night conversations. Movies, music, random complaints about faculties, and conversations that somehow managed to go in every possible direction.

Somewhere along the way, you also became the faculty of the legendary Math 7 course. (Still haven't received a class, by the way. Starting to think the university needs to look into this.)

And of course, how could we forget our rather questionable collection of romantic horror movies? La La Land, 500 Days of Summer , Me Before You , Atonement , and, of course, Pride and Prejudice . A fair amount of discussion, a few disagreements, and some surprisingly good conversations came out of those times.

There were the little things too. The music, the reels, the moon, the random conversations that stretched into the night. Looking back, those were some time well spent. Not because anything extraordinary happened, but because nothing particularly extraordinary did. We just talked, without any rhyme or reason, and

Somehow that was enough to make those moments memorable.

CHAPTER 05

A Darcy-Sized Problem

If I had to pick a line from a movie to describe how I feel right now, it would probably be from Pride and Prejudice.

"You have bewitched me."

I don't want to say I fell for you the day I first saw you. It wasn't your eyes, your looks, or any one particular thing that made it happen.

Somewhere over the semester break, through all the Math 7 jokes, the random reels back and forth, and all the conversations in between, I started falling for you.

Don't know why. Don't know when.

But you gradually became the face I kept looking for in a crowd, just to catch a glimpse of you.

CHAPTER 06

The Thing I Wanted You to Know

If you're still wondering what all of this was about, here's the short version.

From a small crush back in my fifth semester to someone I genuinely wanted to reach out to and get to know better, you somehow became the person who made me want to step outside my comfort zone.

From the guy who rarely talked to anyone unless he had to, to someone who's been fighting his own emotions just to tell the person he likes how he feels. Funny how much can change without either of us really noticing. You probably don't even realise it, but you've been part of that change. And even though I can't quite explain when or why it happened, I think there's something rather beautiful about that.

Although, apparently, I'm still not very good at saying things directly. But hey, a website is a pretty nice attempt, right?

Jokes aside,

I like you.

A lot.

I don't know if you feel the same way, and I'm not expecting you to. You don't owe me an answer, and you certainly don't have to feel the same just because I've told you how I feel. I just wanted to share this with you, and this seemed like the best way I could.

I know telling you this might change things between us, and that's something I've thought about. I genuinely value the connection we've built.

But somewhere along the way, I realised I liked you as more than just a friend, and I didn't want to keep pretending otherwise.

Whatever you make of this, I hope you know that I didn't make this to put you in a difficult position. I made it because I didn't want to look back years from now and wonder what might have happened if I'd just found the courage to say how I felt.

For once, I did.

Whatever happens from here, at least I won't have to wonder about that anymore.
